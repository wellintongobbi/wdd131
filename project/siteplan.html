Site Plan - Gold Aluminum

Site Name

Gold Aluminum

This name was chosen because it represents quality, durability, and premium service. The word "Gold" conveys high value and excellence, aligning with the company's brand.

Optional domain: goldaluminum.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to present the services offered by Gold Aluminum, including aluminum fabrication and installation. It will also serve as a portfolio to showcase past projects and attract new clients.

Scenarios

  • What types of aluminum services does the company offer?
  • How can I request a quote or contact the company?

Color Schema

  • #d4af37 (gold): used for headings and highlights
  • #1a1a1a (black): used for text and accents
  • #f5f5f5 (light gray): used as background color

Typography

  • Poppins: used for headings
  • Roboto: used for body text
